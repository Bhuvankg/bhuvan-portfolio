Family Dining In Kampalapura

Rooted in Flavour. Driven by Warmth.

Located right along the bustling BM Road highway in Kampalapura, Karnataka, Puthari Restaurant offers hearty meals and welcoming dining for families, local residents, and travelers heading between Mysore and Kodagu (Coorg).

Whether you are pulling in after a long highway drive craving hot dum biriyani and spicy egg chilli, or gathering the family for a relaxed evening meal, our dining space is designed to give you comfort, honest portions, and refreshing roadside convenience.
