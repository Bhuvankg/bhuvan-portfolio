Dining Philosophy

Flavourful Food & A Warm Ambiance

Nosh Resto Cafe was conceived as an inviting culinary destination where quality cooking meets the vibrant energy of a contemporary café. Located in Panchavalli along the busy Thalassery–Mysore Road, we serve travelers, families, and local residents seeking fresh, tasty meals in a comfortable setting.

Our kitchen emphasizes freshly prepared dishes with distinct regional spices and high-quality ingredients. Whether you are looking for a complete South Indian or Malabar meal, fragrant dum biriyani, sizzling starters, or simply a hot cup of coffee or cold blend cooler, Nosh has something delicious for every palate.
