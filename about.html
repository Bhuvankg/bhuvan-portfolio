About Puthari Restaurant

A dedicated family dining stop on BM Road, where authentic flavours, generous hospitality, and highway convenience come together.

Our Philosophy

Comfortable Dining For Every Journey

Situated along the vital Bangalore–Mangalore/Mysore–Coorg highway stretch on BM Road in Kampalapura, Karnataka (571107), Puthari Restaurant was established to give travelers and local families a welcoming, clean, and flavour-packed place to eat.

The name Puthari evokes the harvest celebration of Karnataka—a symbol of abundance, sharing freshly gathered food, and bringing communities and families together over celebratory meals. We strive to bring that same warm spirit to every plate we serve.

Whether you are passing through on a weekend road trip, coming home from work, or bringing parents and children for dinner, our dining room provides a relaxed atmosphere away from the highway rush.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where exactly is Puthari Restaurant located?

We are located on BM Road in Kampalapura, Karnataka 571107. Our Google Maps Plus Code is 85P2+2W Kampalapura.

What are your operating hours?

Our business listing indicates opening daily with closing at 2:00 AM. As hours may vary slightly on special holidays, we recommend verifying or reaching out if you are arriving very late.
